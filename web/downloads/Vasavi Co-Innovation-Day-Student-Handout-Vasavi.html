Your Challenge

Work in a team of exactly six to solve a real challenge connected to student wellbeing and campus services. Understand the problem, design a focused idea, create a prototype and explain the impact.

This is not a coding competition. A simple, user-centred prototype that works and is explained clearly can be stronger than a complex unfinished build.

The Scenario

A mid-sized university has a student wellbeing crisis it cannot fully see. Mental health referrals are up 40%, support is fragmented across 12 departments and students can wait three weeks for a first appointment.

Choose One Challenge Track
01 · Student Triage & Routing

Help students reach the right support quickly.

02 · Early Warning System

Spot students who may need help before crisis.

03 · Joined-Up Case Management

Connect support teams around a shared view.

04 · Self-Service Support Portal

Make finding support simple and human.

05 · Wellbeing Intelligence Dashboard

Surface patterns so university leaders can act earlier.
